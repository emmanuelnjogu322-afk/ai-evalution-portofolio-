# Case 005 — Response Ranking & Calibration

**Status:** Completed practice evaluation  
**Focus:** Comparative response evaluation and confidence calibration

## Recorded result

The responses were ranked:

1. **B**
2. **A**
3. **C**

The evaluation exercise recorded evaluator performance of approximately **8.7/10**.

## Calibration lesson

A key issue was distinguishing:

- confidence in an individual evaluation, from
- confidence in the ranking/order of multiple responses.

Recorded individual confidence levels were:

- Response A — **80%**
- Response B — **90%**
- Response C — **50%**

These percentages should not automatically be interpreted as one confidence score for the overall ranking.

## What I learned

When comparing several AI responses, I need to keep separate:

**Individual judgment → comparative judgment → final ranking → confidence in the ranking**

This prevents confidence attached to individual observations from being confused with confidence in the final aggregate conclusion.

## Evaluator takeaway

A strong evaluator needs both **judgment and calibration**. Being able to choose an ordering is not enough; I also need to communicate uncertainty appropriately.
