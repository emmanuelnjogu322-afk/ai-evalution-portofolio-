# Case Study 001 — Remote Work Productivity & AI Claims

**Status:** Completed practice evaluation  
**Type:** Claim verification / evidence assessment

## Task

Evaluate an AI-generated response discussing a claim that remote work increases productivity by approximately 25%.

## Evaluation focus

- Support for the numerical claim
- Correlation versus causation
- Population and context
- Methodology and limitations
- Generalization beyond the evidence
- Confidence calibration

## Finding

The response combined multiple pieces of evidence into a broad productivity claim without adequately establishing that the evidence supported the exact **25%** figure in the stated context.

## Lessons

1. A plausible claim is not automatically a verified claim.
2. Evidence from one population or context does not automatically support another.
3. Numerical claims require attention to methodology and measurement.
4. Evaluators should separate evidence from interpretation.
5. Confidence should decrease when important methodological information is missing.
