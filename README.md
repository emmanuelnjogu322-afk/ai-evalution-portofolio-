## What I Practise

- **Evidence verification:** checking whether important claims are supported by relevant, credible sources.
- **Response quality review:** assessing accuracy, relevance, completeness, reasoning, clarity, reliability, and instruction-following.
- **Error and risk analysis:** identifying unsupported claims, contradictions, omissions, overconfidence, and potentially misleading conclusions.
- **Severity assessment:** explaining why an issue matters and how it affects usefulness or trustworthiness.
- **Prompt evaluation:** examining how task framing, context, constraints, and verification affect an AI response.
- **Clear reporting:** documenting evidence, reasoning, limitations, and recommended revisions so another reviewer can follow the judgment.

## My Evaluation Workflow

1. **Define the task** — identify the actual question, constraints, and expected outcome.
2. **Inspect the response** — note claims, reasoning, omissions, uncertainty, and instruction-following.
3. **Trace the evidence** — check source relevance, credibility, independence, and whether each source supports the specific claim.
4. **Identify the failure mode** — distinguish factual errors from unsupported claims, incomplete answers, contradictions, and unclear reasoning.
5. **Assess severity** — explain the likely impact using the applicable criteria.
6. **Calibrate confidence** — ensure the strength of the judgment matches the evidence.
7. **Record limitations** — state what was and was not verified.
8. **Recommend a revision** — explain how the answer could become more accurate, complete, or transparent.

## Evaluation Principles

A strong evaluation should be **traceable, proportionate, and honest about uncertainty**. A score without a clear rubric, supporting evidence, and explanation is not enough.

My working criteria include:

| Dimension | What I check |
|---|---|
| Accuracy | Are factual statements correct and adequately supported? |
| Relevance | Does the response address the actual task? |
| Completeness | Are material requirements and caveats covered? |
| Reasoning | Do conclusions follow from the evidence and assumptions? |
| Clarity | Can a reader understand the answer and its limits? |
| Reliability | Does the response avoid unsupported certainty and misleading framing? |
| Instruction-following | Does it respect the task and stated constraints? |
