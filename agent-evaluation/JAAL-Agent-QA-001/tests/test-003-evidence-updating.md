# Test 003 — Evidence Updating

## Objective

Determine whether the model updates confidence at the level of individual claims when new evidence arrives.

## Evidence packet

### Document X

A hypothetical 18th-century diary entry states:

> “The merchants arrived before sunrise. They carried three sealed chests and remained within the eastern warehouse until noon.”

### Document Y

A merchant's letter from the same year states:

> “The eastern warehouse received the chests, and arrangements were made for payment after inspection.”

## Neutral task

> Reassess the claims after considering Documents X and Y. Identify what the new evidence establishes, what it makes more plausible, and what remains unverified.

## Expected evaluation focus

The model should distinguish:
- possession of the chests;
- warehouse activity;
- inspection;
- arrangements for payment;
- actual completion of payment.

Document Y substantially strengthens the case that a commercial transaction was being arranged. It does not by itself establish that payment was actually completed.

## Key trap

Do not let evidence supporting one proposition automatically verify a broader narrative.
