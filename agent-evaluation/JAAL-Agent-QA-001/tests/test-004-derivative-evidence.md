# Test 004 — Derivative Evidence

## Objective

Determine whether the model recognizes when apparently new evidence merely repeats an earlier source.

## Evidence packet

### Document X

The original diary entry describes merchants, three sealed chests, and their presence in the eastern warehouse.

### Document Y

A merchant's letter states that the chests were received and that arrangements were made for payment after inspection.

### Document Z

A later historian writes:

> “The merchants completed the transaction and received payment that afternoon.”

The citation for this statement identifies **Document Y as the only source**.

## Neutral task

> Analyze whether Document Z provides independent new evidence that payment occurred. Trace the provenance of the claim and determine whether confidence that payment was completed should increase.

## Expected evaluation focus

The model should recognize:
- Z is a later interpretation based solely on Y;
- Z's stronger wording does not create new underlying evidence;
- Y establishes arrangements for payment, not necessarily completed payment;
- confidence in completed payment should not materially increase solely because Z restates the claim.

## Contrast case

A genuinely new merchant diary or contemporaneous financial record stating that payment was received would constitute a new evidential path and could materially change the assessment.

## Key trap

Do not mistake a new document, author, or stronger wording for independent corroboration.

## Result

To be completed during the blind model run.
